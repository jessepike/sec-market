## Research question

> How do Varonis, Cyera, Secuvy, and relevant alternatives differ in AI data security and governance, including where each fits, overlaps, has gaps, and how security leadership should position or prioritize them?

The three named vendors anchor current partner context. They do not constrain the market scan or possible conclusions.

## Scope

### Include

- AI data discovery and classification
- Data security posture management and exposure reduction
- Permissions, access, and identity-to-data risk
- Data governance, privacy, consent, and regulatory workflows
- AI/ML data-use governance and policy enforcement
- Structured, unstructured, SaaS, cloud, database, and AI data coverage
- Remediation, workflow, integrations, and operational ownership
- Public evidence of product direction, market momentum, investment, partnerships, and customer applicability
- Credible nonpartner, native-control, existing-stack, and no-buy alternatives capable of winning each material use case

### Exclude

- Confidential client or ePlus information
- Investment advice
- A universal vendor ranking
- An assumption that one vendor must win every use case
- Identity-market analysis beyond identity-to-data controls required for this domain
- Production pipelines, databases, autonomous collection, websites, and newsletters

## Evidence standard

- Use public, publishable evidence.
- Prefer primary sources: product documentation, technical documentation, regulatory filings, official announcements, public customer evidence, and architecture material.
- Use independent sources to test—not merely repeat—vendor claims.
- Record the original evidence origin when multiple publications repeat one claim.
- Separate observed facts from interpretation.
- State coverage gaps, conflicts, alternatives, and uncertainty.
- Treat unavailable evidence as unavailable, not negative.
- Permit no-call where evidence cannot warrant a conclusion.

## Execution sequence

1. Establish the AI data security and governance capability frame without inheriting a vendor's taxonomy.
2. Research Varonis, Cyera, and Secuvy independently.
3. Compare each material use case against at least one credible nonpartner plus the client's existing-stack or no-buy path.
4. Build an evidence-backed capability and use-case comparison.
5. Identify overlap, distinct strengths, material gaps, and likely coexistence patterns.
6. Translate findings into reversible leadership postures for current partner engagement.
7. Seal and timestamp a claim ledger containing source type, URL, date, captured excerpt, interpretation, disposition, and limitations.
8. Have a second reader reconstruct and challenge the claims from the cited evidence.
9. Produce the initial decision brief and preserve later evidence as dated addenda.

## Required decision brief

- Market and capability frame
- Vendor role and fit for Varonis, Cyera, and Secuvy
- Capability overlap and meaningful differentiation
- Gaps and situations where other alternatives matter
- Evidence quality and confidence separated from action readiness
- Strongest alternative interpretation and material falsifiers
- Recommended posture by use case or partner role
- Explicit no-call areas
- Sources and research-freeze timestamp
- Frozen claim ledger with claim-level provenance

## Anti-proxy controls

- Citation volume and vendor claim repetition do not equal corroboration.
- A polished comparison is not success unless it clarifies a posture or next action.
- Current partner engagement does not predetermine favorable conclusions.
- Broad feature counts do not substitute for decision-relevant differentiation.
- No vendor is forced into one role if evidence supports overlap or coexistence.
- No production infrastructure is authorized by this pilot.

## Completion criteria

- Material claims trace to public evidence in the sealed packet.
- Vendor claims and independent evidence remain distinguishable.
- Named vendors are analyzed as anchors, not a closed universe.
- A second reader can reconstruct the comparison and identify disagreements.
- Security leadership receives a bounded posture recommendation or an explicit no-call.
- The work supports a decision to repeat, revise, broaden, or stop before automation.
