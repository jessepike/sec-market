## Section read

Platforms that finished buying identity are now seeding and buying agent control. In one five-week window, six platform actors moved on agent identity and control: Akamai into Hush, Cisco into Zafran, CrowdStrike Falcon Fund into Above Security, plus Cisco/Astrix, Zscaler/Symmetry intent, and Palo Alto/Portkey — half already on this board.

Agent governance got funded at every stage in the same window — seed through Series C plus undisclosed strategic checks — not a formation story alone. Zenity is a Series C; Onyx a Series B; Arrakis, Actualyze, and SYN portfolio names are seeds. That stage spread is the signature of a category assembling.

Capital heat is arriving ahead of disclosed revenue. Identity and AI Runtime stay WATCH on the Tempo board with low evidence strength; the heat chips are capital, not buyer spend. **Falsifier:** the first disclosed agentic-identity revenue print decides whether this category is real spend or product naming (see Watch next).

This is capital activity. It does not change the published infrastructure-lag market call.
