### A1 — NVIDIA marks the compute-cycle inflection

- **Period:** 2023-04-30 through 2026-04-26
- **Source class:** IR / FILING
- **Sources:** [NVIDIA Q1 FY24](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2023/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-First-Quarter-Fiscal-2024/), [NVIDIA Q1 FY27](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-First-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/), [Q1 FY27 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000052/nvda-20260426.htm)

## Layer D/E — Buyer demand, deployment, and forcing evidence

### D1 — Security budgets did not accelerate with the 2024–2025 infrastructure cycle

- **Period:** 2023 through 2025
- **Source class:** INDEPENDENT
- **Sources:** [IANS 2023](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/new-research-from-ians-and-artico-search-reveals-cybersecurity-budgets-increased-just-6-for-2022-2023-cycle-301938338.html), [IANS 2024](https://www.iansresearch.com/resources/press-releases/detail/new-research-reveals-security-budgets-only-increased-2-points-in-2024--while-12--of-cisos-faced-reductions), [IANS 2025](https://sf-cdn.iansresearch.com/sitefinity/docs/default-source/reports/2025-security-budget-benchmark-summary-report_08_05_2025.pdf?sfvrsn=43097214_2)
- **Frozen evidence:** average security-budget growth was 6 percent in 2023, 8 percent in 2024, and 4 percent in 2025. The consistent series published in the 2025 report put security at 11.6 percent of IT budgets in 2023, 11.9 percent in 2024, and 10.9 percent in 2025. IANS said 2025 was the first year in five years that IT spending growth exceeded security-budget growth and linked the reversal to AI and cloud-infrastructure investment.
- **Disposition:** strongest buyer-side evidence of a delayed or absent total-budget response
- **Signal:** total security-budget response weakened through 2025
- **Limit:** self-reported survey; AI/cloud attribution is IANS interpretation rather than a disclosed line-item split. The contemporaneous 2024 release reported 13.2 percent of IT; the 2025 report restated 2024 to 11.9 percent under its consistent historical methodology. This episode uses the later internally consistent series rather than splicing the two methodologies.
