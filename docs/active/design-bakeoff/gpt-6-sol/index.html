      How to read this record

      Figures are presented as each company reported them. Revenue growth, company-defined metrics, funding and ownership are different measures, and periods differ. A link on every figure opens its underlying source.

      Suppliers and domains are shown alphabetically. A supplier with no figure has no comparable figure in this tracker; that does not mean the company has no revenue. The 30-day record includes both company events and SEC filings, so an earnings release and its filing can appear separately.
