    What is covered

    Private companies publish no comparable quarterly revenue. Their rows carry the facts they do publish: the last funding round, the company's own stated metric, and ownership. Segments of larger companies are named with their parent, and no separate revenue is shown because none is disclosed.

    What is not covered

      Sources and method

      Where the figures come from

      Revenue and growth come from SEC filings (10-Q, 10-K, 8-K, 6-K) and the company's own results release. Funding, acquisitions, leadership changes, debt and restructuring come from filings, company statements and wire releases. An item found only in trade press is held and listed under collection status, not published.

      How to read a figure

      Every number is a link. Hover or focus it to see the document's title, publisher, publication date and the period it covers; click to open the document. Growth is year over year as the company reported it. Dollar figures are abbreviated (B, M) and otherwise shown as reported.

      What the machine does not do

      It does not rank, score, forecast or colour anything good or bad. Lists are alphabetical unless you change the order. Any judgment appears only in the owner's signed read, and there is none this week.
