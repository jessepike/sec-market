Security Market Pulse · front page redesign · 2 Oct 2026

Three models, one brief, three fresh front pages

GPT-6 Sol, GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 each researched on their own and built a working prototype on the real tracker data (26 Sep 2026 snapshot). The images are the first screen a reader sees, at desktop and phone width. Open each prototype to scroll and click it for real.

Recommendation: build from Opus 5.5, with Astra's calm spacing and type.

Opus is the only one where a security leader gets a picture of the market on the first screen: six event counts with twelve-month trends, the full 30-day change list, and a timeline of every event. Astra is the most pleasant to look at but hides almost everything behind a click. Sol reads like a marketing landing page and shows four items before you scroll.

Claude Opus 5.5

Recommended base

A calm changelog of the market with counts and a timeline beside it.

What a reader gets

  • Six event counts (deals, funding, results, restructuring, debt, leadership) with monthly bars.
  • All 25 changes from the last 30 days, each with its source.
  • A twelve-month timeline, one dot per event, by type.
  • Private funding rounds, ownership changes, a public-company table with SEC revenue sparklines, then twelve compact domain cards.
  • Clicking any supplier opens a side panel with all its sources and history.
  • A dashed, empty "Owner's read" slot, reserved for your signed words.

Watch for

  • Dense below the fold; needs Astra-style breathing room.
  • One count ("16 SEC filers") is typed in, not derived. Fixable in the build.
