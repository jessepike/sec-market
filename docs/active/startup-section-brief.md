## Hard read

The packet is **enough raw material**. It is **not world-class if we put 58 companies on the page**.

Almost every dated raise comes from Return on Security’s last five weekly posts, plus one Cybersecurity Ventures recap. Specialist VC pages added a handful of names without amounts. A sharp person in San Francisco will smell a reprint. We only win if we **attribute the feed**, **reconcile conflicting numbers**, and **say what it means**.

Do not ship the table. The table is the evidence drawer.

---

## Insights I would stand behind

These are provisional. They are not a market call and not investment advice.

1. **The new company formation is in governing AI agents.** Zenity, Onyx, Hush, Arrakis, Arcade, CodeIntegrity. This is not “AI security” as a slogan. It is runtime control, identity, and authorization for agents that can act.
2. **Identity is being rebuilt for non-human access.** Oak’s $60M seed, Way’s $20M seed, Hush with Akamai on the cap table, Keyfactor’s $1B private-equity check. Buyers are in pain now, not in a research cycle.
3. **Money is barbell.** Huge late checks (Horizon3.ai, Cathedral, ThreatLocker, Spur) and a lot of seed in agents/identity. The interesting story is not “cyber is hot.” It is where the *new* names are forming versus where the *old* platforms are still raising.

## What I would not claim

- That we mapped the whole market
- That a portfolio “2026” line happened this month
- A single number when sources disagree (Glow, Neo, Zenity)
- That a $116K SEC filing belongs on the front of the site
- That we discovered these rounds ourselves

## How it should feel

Same discipline as the rest of Pulse: one edition, headline first, proof in the drawer.

**First screen**

- One short read (3–4 sentences): agents + identity, barbell capital, we are not calling a boom
- Five headlines only. Company, what they do, amount if we trust it, why it matters
- Three heat chips: Agent governance · Identity / NHI · Exposure / autonomous testing
- A smaller “just appeared” row for names we know exist but cannot price (Arcade, Team8 builds)

**Behind the fold**

- The rest of the dated raises, grouped by week
- Conflicting amounts shown as a conflict, not averaged
- Source, date, excerpt

**Never on the first screen**

- 58 rows
- Micro-filings
- Mature recap names unless the *2026 check* is the news
- M&A (parked)
