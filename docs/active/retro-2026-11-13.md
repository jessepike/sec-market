# Why releases kept failing, Nov 2 to 13 (blameless review)

The site published on Nov 10 and then not again until a fix was promoted on Nov 13. Measured from
the Actions run list for Nov 2 to 13: 9 failed gate runs, 6 failed or cut-off collections, about 40
failed publisher runs (36 of them a restart loop on Nov 12), 16 failed runtime canaries and 23
failed freshness probes.

## What changed in how the loop works

- Detection is no longer the end: each doctor case either takes a capped step or hands to the repair step with the evidence.
- Preview CI is watched, so a broken test is fixed the day it breaks, not when the gate finds it.
- The daily 08:43 loop runs the repair runbook when the release failed instead of only reporting it.
