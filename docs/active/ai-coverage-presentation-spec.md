# AI security coverage — presentation

**ID:** `SEC-MARKET-AI-002` · **Version:** v0.1 · **Opened:** 2026-08-28
**Depends on:** `SEC-MARKET-AI-001` Part 2, recorded at `docs/active/ai-coverage-gather-2026-08-27.md`

## Why

The gather asked twelve public companies what they disclose about AI security revenue and all twelve
disclose nothing. The evidence that answers the reader's complaint sits one layer down in the same
filings, in the acquisition accounting: platforms are paying disclosed prices for AI security
companies whose revenue those same filings call immaterial, and assigning nothing to customer
relationships while doing it.

This spec publishes that read. It uses shapes the site already has. No new component, no new domain
card, no layout change, and no posture moves.

## Change 1 — five evidence records

Append to `web/src/data/evidence.ts` using ids `D32` through `D36`, matching the shape of the
existing records exactly. All five are `sourceClass: 'FILING'`, which is the strongest class the
site has. `conflictingEvidenceIds: []` on all five.

| id | Covers | Source |
|---|---|---|
| D32 | Protect AI purchase consideration and its allocation | A |
| D33 | Palo Alto FY25 acquisitions immateriality statement | A |
| D34 | CalypsoAI close, price, allocation and immateriality | B |
| D35 | SurePath AI close, price and immateriality | B |
| D36 | CyberArk deregistration | C |

`interpretation` must state what the record supports without overreaching. `limitations` must carry
the two bounds, on whichever records they apply to:

- "Not material" is an accounting threshold relative to the acquirer's own size. It is an upper
  bound on the acquired revenue, not a statement that it is zero. Palo Alto's revenue base is far
  larger than F5's, so the same phrase bounds a larger number there.
- An absent customer-relationships intangible means the acquirer assigned no separable value to
  customer contracts. It is a valuation judgment, not a customer count.

D33's `limitations` must also say that the immateriality sentence covers the fiscal year's
acquisitions collectively rather than naming Protect AI alone. D32's `interpretation` carries the
QRadar contrast, because both figures are in that one filing.

## Change 4 — fix the list join before it ships broken

`hypotheses.ts` builds the absorption note with `.join(' and ')`. With two items that reads fine.
With five it produces "Wiz in Cloud and CyberArk in Identity and Protect AI in AI runtime and
CalypsoAI in AI runtime and SurePath AI in AI runtime". Add a small helper that renders a list as
"A, B, C, D and E" and use it for both `note` and `firstTest`. No gate would have caught this.

The hypothesis state stays `TRACKING`. It does not move to `SUPPORTED`. More absorptions strengthen
the evidence, and promoting a tracked hypothesis is a judgment that gets its own decision, not a
side effect of loading data.
