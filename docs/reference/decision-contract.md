## Candidate posture contract

The following posture set governs the first episode:

- **Investigate:** authorize one bounded inquiry to resolve a named uncertainty.
- **Prepare:** take a reversible readiness action before stronger demand evidence arrives.
- **Prioritize:** allocate near-term leadership attention or resources over genuine alternatives.
- **Defer:** make no near-term allocation and state the evidence that would reopen the decision.
- **Watch:** monitor named indicators on a stated cadence without active preparation.
- **Unchanged:** retain the pre-existing posture for stated reasons.
- **No-call:** evidence cannot responsibly support a posture judgment.

“Watch,” “unchanged,” and “no-call” are distinct. Watch has explicit monitoring commitments, unchanged preserves an existing posture, and no-call states that the evidence cannot warrant one.

## Evidence boundary

### Research freeze

- **Decision:** timestamp the initial evidence packet when the bounded research pass is sealed
- **Rule:** the initial judgment may use only evidence captured in that sealed packet
- **Revision rule:** later evidence belongs in a dated addendum and must not be backfilled into the initial judgment

### Research budget and stop rule

- **Decision:** one bounded manual research pass with no production adapters, database, paid-source procurement, or autonomous collection system
- **Stop conditions:** enough independent evidence exists to form a warranted claim; the evidence supports an honest no-call; or the research budget is exhausted
- **Prohibited behavior:** expanding source breadth merely to avoid a no-call

### Public and private records

- **Decision:** public evidence and publishable intelligence core; private decision-effect record containing the sealed baseline, owner feedback, and resulting posture
- **Boundary:** no client-identifying or confidential ePlus information enters the public core

## Observation contract

### Reasoned uptake or no effect

- **Decision:** use the before-and-after decision record below
- **Before delivery:** record the sealed baseline posture, decision criteria, timing expectation, and intended next action
- **After delivery:** record whether the judgment changed understanding, criteria, timing, posture, or next action
- **Valid result:** a specific explanation that the judgment had no effect
- **Invalid proxy:** delivery, readership, agreement, or occurrence of a conversation alone

### Reconstructability

- A second reader must identify the supported claim, posture, uncertainty, strongest alternative, and reversal condition using the cited evidence.
- Disagreement must be recorded rather than reconciled silently.
