## Finite path to 100%

1. **Lock the meaningful inventory.** Reconcile the parent's claim-level definition into a versioned supplier/claim catalog. Record required components, entity/segment scope, currency, reporting period, authority and freshness interval. Daily latest news/discovery and financial disclosures; weekly ownership/leadership/latest funding; monthly profile are proposals to review. Keep immutable historical fact provenance separate from rolling latestness. Every exclusion must be a source-backed applicability rule, not a failed-source escape. A verified undisclosed answer may count only when that specific claim explicitly allows a disclosure-status answer. Do not delete currently populated optional qualifiers to pass. Keep old/new denominators and catalog change reasons in the receipt.
2. **Build the independent proof adapters.** Structured SEC adapter maps the validated concept, exact dollars, quarter start/end, comparable prior year, filing date and scope into exact candidate-value hashes plus source body hash/locator. Public-document adapter checks actual retained source bytes against specific claim values and qualifiers using structured extraction or exact excerpts, not HTTP status or a model's narrative completion label. It produces the protected typed certificate; collector cannot write it. Add wrong-company, wrong-quarter, currency-basis, segment-v-company, mismatched quote, stale/future timestamp, contradictory source, dropped qualifier and silent-null fixtures. Typed evidence links to the exact claim and substantive locator, with verifiedAt separate from fetch/attempt times.
3. **Fill the latestness gaps.** Reuse successful research and immutable proofs; preserve their timestamps. Track discovery coverage since the previous safe watermark through an explicit cutoff, with pagination/feed scope. The earlier Google/Microsoft feed checks cover only recent days; that does not close the earlier interval. Resolve each missing interval with primary company/parent archives, paginated releases, IR feeds, official transaction announcements, SEC filings or a valid primary wire-service copy. A homepage or filings list is not proof of a specific value or absence of newer facts.
4. **Work the exception inventory to zero required failures.** The actual previous attempt has 37 suppliers with failed source/currentness checks and 14 undisclosed-answer rows needing explicit policy. Export one actionable supplier/claim row per blocker: exact source, HTTP/content issue, missing interval/claim component, supported alternate source, next retry and proof acceptance condition. Retry public alternatives, not expanded credentials or lowered standards. A source retrieval failure remains visible even when a valid alternate substantiates the required claim. Actual unresolvable required claims keep the refresh held; report the specific blocker rather than claiming success.
5. **Run one transaction and certify the whole snapshot.** Gather candidate edits in the isolated runner; validate literal-only changes, exact value/period/source mappings and all required freshness windows. Regenerating a receipt cannot renew old proof timestamps. On failure preserve the candidate/exception receipt and restore active published facts and complete date. Persist the failed-attempt status separately for owner delivery; it is not a data update. On 100% prepare the complete-refresh timestamp, candidate hashes, field-level change summary and source/coverage breakdown; select the certified tracker snapshot atomically.
6. **Publish and verify the actual result.** Run all data, built-artifact and desktop/phone browser checks; verify signed issue/history integrity and inspect before/after renders. Use preview -> approved exact main SHA -> Vercel Ready. Compare the public receipt and snapshot hashes to the prepared candidate. Deliver one data-updated message with the complete date, actual changes, unchanged checks, scope/denominator and evidence. Blocker notifications remain a separate class; never send a data-updated notification for an attempted or merely prepared candidate.

The v1 gate currently has 518 conservative required supplier/group bundles with pinned components and no silent exclusions; it is not the final public score denominator. Existing legacy receipts do not contain independent typed certificates, so 0 bundles are formally certified under this new contract. That does not negate the prior 551 successful-status properties or mean no research happened. No data refresh is marked complete at this point. No arbitrary green thresholds, broader platform or scope reduction is proposed.
