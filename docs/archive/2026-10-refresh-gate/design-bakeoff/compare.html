Security Market Pulse · front page redesign · 2 Oct 2026

Four models, one brief, four fresh front pages

Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Fable 5.1, GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Astra each researched on their own and built a working prototype on the real tracker data (26 Sep 2026 snapshot). The images are the first screen a reader sees, at desktop and phone width. Open each prototype to scroll and click it for real.

Recommendation: build from Opus 5.5, add Fable's growth chart to the first screen, and take Sol's fact layout and Astra's phone economy.

Four models then reviewed all four designs blind, labelled A to D. Three of four picked the Opus design as the winner, including Fable and Sol, who both ranked it above their own. Astra picked its own. Every reviewer flagged the same must-fix in the Opus design: the per-domain "median growth" badges have no source or period and have to go.

Claude Opus 5.5

Recommended base

A calm changelog of the market with counts and a timeline beside it.

Desktop 6,235 pxPhone ≈11,350 pxSaw its own render

What a reader gets

  • Six event counts (deals, funding, results, restructuring, debt, leadership) with monthly bars.
  • All 25 changes from the last 30 days, each with its source.
  • A twelve-month timeline, one dot per event, by type.
  • Private funding rounds, ownership changes, a public-company table with SEC revenue sparklines, then twelve compact domain cards.
  • Clicking any supplier opens a side panel with all its sources and history.
  • A dashed, empty "Owner's read" slot, reserved for your signed words.

Watch for

  • All four reviewers: the per-domain "median growth" badges have no source or period and must go.
  • Timeline dots are 8 px and mouse-only; phone page is the longest at 11,352 px.
  • Says 16 SEC filers are complete; the data holds 14. Count is typed in, not derived.

Claude Fable 5.1

Runner-up

A ledger of dated events beside a chart of every public supplier's reported growth.

Desktop 5,346 pxPhone 6,090 pxSaw its own render

What a reader gets

  • One line of counts: 74 suppliers, 18 public, 22 private, 95 dated events, 144 source documents.
  • The 30-day change list with filter chips by type.
  • A dot chart of the latest quarter's revenue growth for every public supplier, with the quarter before as a hollow dot. Alphabetical, sortable by value.
  • A board of all twelve domains, one fact line per supplier, with revenue trend lines for SEC filers.

Watch for

  • Austere; reads more like a ledger than a product.
  • Dropped "implied" from Upwind's valuation, rounds SEC figures in hover text, and leaves 8 SEC filings out of the change list.
